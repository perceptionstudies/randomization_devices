Nggam Spider Divination

In Mambila culture, a diviner places flat oracle cards around the entrance of a spider's burrow, then covers the hole. The diviner reads the way in which the spider disturbs and overturns the cards, providing an answer to a question.

The burrow is undisturbed. Place the cards to begin.

The Kleroterion

Bronze name-tablets (pinakia) slot into rows on a stone slab. A hollow tube on the side holds a mixed column of black and white dice — one exits per row. A white die indicates the citizens in the row are chosen; a black die indicates they are not.

The machine awaits.

Scapulimancy

A diviner observes as a shoulder blade is placed on a live fire. Cracks begin to form; the diviner reads their direction, branching, and curvature to make decisions.

The bone is cold. Apply heat to begin divination.

Lavarand

Cloudflare's San Francisco office films a wall of lava lamps. The lava lamps' motion is truly random, and a video feed of the lamps seeds the cryptographic random number generators.
